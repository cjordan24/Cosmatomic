Each species row lists its value, uncertainty and source.
- **43b-thermochemistry-700K.png** — Same reaction at 700 K: ΔrG° becomes positive (K < 1). This is why industrial ammonia synthesis trades yield for rate, and the panel warns that the constant-ΔH/ΔS approximation applies.

TiO₆ coordination is derived, and the Bragg table marks systematic absences.

- **45c-material-properties-water.png** — Water at 293 K and 1 atm: speed of sound, density, cp, viscosity and thermal conductivity from the NIST reference equation of state (IAPWS-95), static permittivity from Malmberg & Maryott (1956), and Daimon & Masumura dispersion.
