- **130a-coriolis.png** — The Coriolis effect (Gate 20.4): a puck sliding straight across a spinning table curves to the right for an observer riding it; at 45° a 10 m/s wind feels 2ωv sin φ = 1.03 × 10⁻³ m/s² (Humphreys 1920; ω from his sidereal day).
- **130b-convection.png** — Convection: Rayleigh’s 1916 threshold for a 1 cm layer of air, 0.0329 (his “one-thirtieth part … about 9° C.”) — a 12 °C difference overturns into rolls.
- **130c-clouds.png** — Clouds: the ten genera at their NWS base heights; cirrus selected, with the NWS definition and Luke Howard’s original (1803, 3rd ed. 1865).
- **130d-water.png** — Where Earth’s water is (USGS after Shiklomanov) and how fast it moves: 12,900 km³ in the air, 495,000 km³ a year through it (NASA) — about 9.5 days each, against the USGS’s “about 10 days”.
- **130e-circulation.png** — The global circulation: Hadley, Ferrel and polar cells with the trade winds, westerlies and jets (NWS); in the Southern Hemisphere lows turn clockwise.
