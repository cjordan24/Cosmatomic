# Phase 5 screenshots

Generated by `tests/e2e/phase5.spec.ts` from the running application (Playwright, Chromium + SwiftShader). Not edited.

- **54a-star-map-overview.png** — Nearby-star map (#/cosmic/stars): every star, brown dwarf and white dwarf within 10 pc (Reylé et al. 2021, 2023 update) in the galactic frame, Sun at the centre, rings every 2.5 pc, drop lines to the Galactic plane, systems listed by distance.
- **54b-proxima-selected.png** — Proxima Centauri selected: catalogue parallax, position, proper motion and radial velocity, each with a NASA ADS link to its original reference; derived distance (1.302 pc / 4.25 ly), galactic l/b and tangential velocity; the M5.5 dwarf calibration (Pecaut & Mamajek) labelled as a typical value, not a measurement.
- **54c-icrs-cns5-20pc.png** — Equatorial (ICRS) frame with the CNS5 layer: stars between 10 and 20 pc (Golovin et al. 2023) as dim points, rings every 5 pc; α Cen A selected: too bright for Gaia, so measured G is unknown and the catalogue’s estimate is shown as an estimate only.
- **55a-cmd.png** — Observational colour–magnitude diagram: absolute G against G−RP for every CNS5 star within 20 pc (Golovin et al. 2023), 10 pc objects coloured by category, main sequence and white-dwarf sequence visible, mean class V sequence overlaid, Hipparcos-converted stars hollow.
- **55b-luminosity-temperature.png** — Luminosity–temperature diagram for 10 pc stars: T_eff from the spectral-type calibration, L scaled by measured absolute magnitude, dashed constant-radius lines from Stefan–Boltzmann, the Sun at the IAU 2015 nominal 5772 K / 1 L☉; Proxima selected.
- **55c-spectral-classes.png** — MK spectral classes O–Y with class V temperature, mass and radius ranges from the Mamajek dwarf sequence, counts of 10 pc objects per class, and the class criteria from the IAU OAE Glossary.
- **56a-proxima-system.png** — Proxima Centauri planets: face-on orbits from published a, e, ω with ±1σ bands on a, Mercury–Jupiter overlaid for scale; Proxima b elements with uncertainties (exoplanet.eu), minimum mass in Earth masses, Kepler-law check, and a flagged periastron time that is not a valid Julian date.
- **56b-gj876-mass-period.png** — GJ 876 (four planets, two of them in resonance) with the mass–period diagram of all planets within 10 pc against the Solar System planets; hollow points are minimum masses.
- **57a-star-thermal-model.png** — Proxima Centauri: Planck model at the M5.5 V mean T_eff (2930 K, peak 989 nm in the near infrared) with the visible range marked, the caveat that real M-dwarf spectra are dominated by molecular bands, and registry links to its class and its blackbody model.
- **57b-atlas-from-star.png** — Spectral atlas opened from Proxima Centauri: the 2930 K Planck curve, the note that it models the continuum at the class mean temperature, and reverse links to every 10 pc star modelled at this temperature.
- **57c-classes-lines.png** — Spectral-class table reached from the Ca II lines in the Spectral atlas, with class G highlighted and each class linked to the NIST line spectra that dominate it (IAU OAE Glossary).
- **58a-search-stars.png** — Universal search now finds nearby stars and exoplanets (the 10 pc catalogue loads on the first query): “Sirius” → Sirius A and B with type, spectral type and distance.
- **58b-sun-crosslinks.png** — Sun info panel: member of class G (IAU OAE: G2), Planck model link, photospheric composition (Asplund et al. 2009 log ε, each element linked to its atomic view) and links to the HR diagram and nearby stars.
