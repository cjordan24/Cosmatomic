VOYAGER VENTURES

Phases 15–19: Computing & Engineering

Following the successful completion of Phase 14, begin developing a fifth major section of Voyager Ventures titled Computing & Engineering.

Live deployment: https://voyager-ventures.vercel.app

This section should eventually rival the existing four major sections in depth, educational value, visual quality, and interactive functionality.

The objective is to help users understand how humanity has applied the fundamental laws of science to create computers, artificial intelligence, machines, vehicles, spacecraft, and other complex engineered systems.

Before implementation:

* Review the current repository, architecture, phase handoffs, and live deployment.

* Establish a roadmap for Phases 15–19.
